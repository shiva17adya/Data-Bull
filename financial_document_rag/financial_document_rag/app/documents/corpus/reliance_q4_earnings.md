# Revenue

Consolidated revenue for the quarter came in at INR 2,41,800 crore, an increase of 12%
year-over-year. Growth was primarily driven by the retail and digital services segments,
which together contributed nearly 48% of total consolidated revenue. The oil-to-chemicals
(O2C) segment posted flat revenue on a sequential basis as refining margins normalized
after an unusually strong prior quarter. Management noted that retail same-store sales
growth accelerated to 9.4% during the quarter, compared to 6.1% in the prior quarter.

# Revenue Growth

On a year-over-year basis, revenue growth was broad-based across segments. Digital
services revenue grew 18% year-over-year on the back of continued subscriber additions
and higher average revenue per user (ARPU), which rose to INR 203 from INR 189 a year
earlier. Retail segment revenue grew 15% year-over-year, supported by store expansion
and higher footfall during the festive shopping period. The O2C segment grew a modest 3%
year-over-year, reflecting softer global demand for petrochemical products.

# Profitability

Consolidated EBITDA for the quarter was INR 44,200 crore, up 9% year-over-year, while
consolidated net profit came in at INR 19,650 crore, up 7% year-over-year. Operating
margin for the quarter stood at 18.3%, a slight contraction of 40 basis points compared
to the same quarter last year, primarily due to higher input costs in the O2C segment and
continued investment in new retail store formats.

# Operating Margin

Segment-level operating margin trends were mixed. The digital services segment posted an
operating margin of 51.2%, up from 49.8% a year earlier, driven by operating leverage as
the subscriber base scaled. The retail segment's operating margin was 8.1%, broadly
stable year-over-year. The O2C segment's operating margin compressed to 11.4% from 13.9%
a year earlier due to elevated feedstock costs.

# Cash Flow

Operating cash flow for the quarter was INR 38,900 crore. Free cash flow, after capital
expenditure, was INR 14,300 crore. The company continued to generate strong cash flow
despite elevated capital spending on network expansion and new energy initiatives.

# Capex

Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 24,600 crore, with the majority allocated to
the new energy business, including solar module manufacturing capacity and battery
storage infrastructure, as well as continued 5G network densification.

# Business Segments

The company reported results across four primary segments: Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C),
Oil and Gas (exploration and production), Digital Services (telecom and connectivity),
and Retail (organized retail and e-commerce). Each segment is managed as an independent
business unit with its own capital allocation targets.

# Management Outlook

Management indicated continued confidence in double-digit consolidated revenue growth
for the coming fiscal year, driven primarily by the digital services and retail segments.
Management also highlighted that the new energy business is expected to begin
contributing meaningfully to consolidated EBITDA within the next 18 to 24 months, as
the first phase of solar manufacturing capacity comes online.

# Risks

The company flagged several risks for investors to consider, including volatility in
global crude oil and petrochemical prices, regulatory changes affecting the telecom
sector, execution risk associated with the large new energy capital expenditure program,
and increased competitive intensity in the organized retail sector from both domestic
and international players.

# Guidance

Management reiterated full-year guidance of consolidated revenue growth in the range of
10% to 13%, with capital expenditure expected to remain elevated at similar levels to the
current fiscal year as new energy investments continue.
