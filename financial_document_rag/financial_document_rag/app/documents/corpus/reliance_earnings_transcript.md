# Revenue

Analyst: Could you walk us through the drivers of the 12% year-over-year revenue growth
this quarter? CFO: Certainly. The largest contributor was our digital services segment,
where ARPU growth and continued subscriber additions drove an 18% year-over-year
increase in segment revenue. Retail also had a very strong quarter, up 15% year-over-year,
helped by festive season demand and the ramp-up of newer store formats.

# Revenue Growth

Analyst: Do you expect this pace of growth to continue into the next fiscal year? CFO:
We are guiding to consolidated revenue growth of 10% to 13% for the coming fiscal year.
We expect digital services and retail to remain the primary growth engines, while O2C
growth will likely stay in the low single digits given the current global demand
environment for petrochemicals.

# Profitability

Analyst: Operating margin contracted slightly this quarter. Can you explain why? CFO:
The contraction was primarily driven by higher feedstock costs in our O2C segment,
which compressed segment margins by roughly 250 basis points. This was partially offset
by margin expansion in digital services, where we continue to benefit from operating
leverage as our subscriber base scales.

# Operating Margin

Analyst: Should we expect O2C margins to recover? CFO: We expect some normalization in
feedstock costs over the next two to three quarters, which should support a gradual
recovery in O2C margins, though we are not providing a specific target at this time.

# Management Outlook

Management: We remain highly confident in our long-term strategy. The new energy
business remains on track, and we expect the first phase of our solar manufacturing
capacity to be commercially operational within the next 18 to 24 months. We continue to
view this as a multi-decade growth opportunity for the group.

# Risks

Analyst: What are the key risks you are monitoring? CFO: We continue to monitor global
crude oil price volatility, regulatory developments in telecom spectrum policy, and
execution risk on our new energy capital projects given their scale and complexity. We
are also watching competitive intensity in organized retail closely.

# Guidance

CFO: To summarize our guidance, we expect full-year consolidated revenue growth in the
range of 10% to 13%, with capital expenditure remaining at levels broadly similar to the
current fiscal year as we continue to invest in new energy and digital infrastructure.
