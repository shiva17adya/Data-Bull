# Revenue

For the full fiscal year, consolidated revenue was INR 9,38,400 crore, an increase of
11% compared to the previous fiscal year. The digital services and retail segments
together accounted for approximately 46% of total revenue, up from 41% three years
earlier, reflecting the company's continued strategic shift away from the legacy
oil-to-chemicals business toward consumer-facing and technology-led segments.

# Revenue Growth

Over the past five fiscal years, consolidated revenue has grown at a compound annual
growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 9.8%. Digital services revenue has grown at a
CAGR of 21% over the same period, while retail segment revenue has grown at a CAGR of
17%. The O2C segment has grown at a more modest CAGR of 4%, consistent with management's
stated strategy of diversifying the group's revenue base.

# Profitability

Full-year consolidated EBITDA was INR 1,71,200 crore, and consolidated net profit was
INR 76,400 crore, representing a net margin of 8.1%. Return on capital employed (ROCE)
for the year was 9.3%, an improvement from 8.6% in the prior year, driven primarily by
margin expansion in the digital services segment.

# Operating Margin

Consolidated operating margin for the fiscal year was 18.2%, broadly stable compared to
the prior year. Management attributes margin stability to the offsetting effects of
margin expansion in digital services and retail against margin compression in the O2C
segment due to global overcapacity in petrochemicals.

# Cash Flow

The company generated operating cash flow of INR 1,52,000 crore for the fiscal year.
After capital expenditure of INR 1,08,000 crore, free cash flow was INR 44,000 crore,
the highest level in the company's history, reflecting improved capital discipline
across legacy segments even as new energy investment continued to ramp up.

# Capex

Total capital expenditure for the fiscal year was INR 1,08,000 crore. Approximately 35%
of this was directed toward the new energy business, 30% toward digital services
network infrastructure, 20% toward retail store and warehouse expansion, and the
remainder toward maintenance capital expenditure in the O2C and oil and gas segments.

# Business Segments

The annual report details four reportable segments: Oil-to-Chemicals, Oil and Gas,
Digital Services, and Retail. A fifth segment, New Energy, is currently reported within
"Others" but management has indicated it will become a separate reportable segment once
it reaches a materiality threshold, expected within the next two fiscal years.

# Management Outlook

Management's letter to shareholders emphasized three strategic priorities for the
coming years: scaling the new energy business into a globally competitive
manufacturing operation, deepening digital services penetration in rural markets, and
expanding the retail footprint into smaller Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. Management
expressed confidence that these initiatives would sustain double-digit consolidated
revenue growth over the medium term.

# Risks

The annual report's risk factors section highlights commodity price volatility,
currency exchange rate exposure given significant capital expenditure denominated in
foreign currency, regulatory and policy risk across telecom and energy sectors,
execution risk on large greenfield projects, and cybersecurity risk given the scale of
the group's digital services subscriber base.

# Guidance

While the annual report does not provide quarterly guidance, management indicated an
aspiration to more than double the contribution of new energy and digital services to
consolidated EBITDA over the next five years.
