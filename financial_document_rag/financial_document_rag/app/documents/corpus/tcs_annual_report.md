# Revenue

For the full fiscal year, TCS reported revenue of USD 29.8 billion, representing
constant-currency growth of 5.8% over the prior fiscal year. In rupee terms, full-year
revenue was INR 2,47,200 crore, up 7.2% year-over-year.

# Revenue Growth

Over the trailing five-year period, TCS has delivered a revenue CAGR of approximately
8.4% in constant currency terms. Growth has been led by the BFSI vertical and by
increasing demand for cloud, data, and artificial intelligence-related services, which
together now represent a growing share of the company's total contract value.

# Profitability

Full-year operating margin was 24.3%, broadly stable compared to the prior fiscal year.
Net profit for the fiscal year was INR 48,650 crore, representing a net margin of
19.7%. Return on equity for the fiscal year was 51.2%, reflecting the company's
capital-light business model and consistent high dividend payout policy.

# Operating Margin

The annual report notes that operating margin has remained within a relatively narrow
band of 24% to 26% over the past several years, with the company balancing wage
inflation and subcontractor costs against pricing discipline and productivity gains
from automation and AI-led delivery.

# Cash Flow

Full-year operating cash flow was INR 52,300 crore, and the company maintained its
policy of returning the substantial majority of free cash flow to shareholders through
dividends and buybacks. The company's balance sheet remains effectively debt-free.

# Business Segments

The annual report breaks down revenue across seven industry verticals: BFSI,
manufacturing, retail and consumer business, technology and services, life sciences and
healthcare, communications and media, and others including public services and
energy/resources/utilities.

# Management Outlook

Management's letter to shareholders emphasized continued investment in artificial
intelligence capabilities, including the expansion of the company's proprietary AI
platform used for internal delivery and client-facing solutions. Management also noted
continued investment in reskilling the workforce to align with evolving client demand
for AI and cloud-native engineering skills.

# Risks

Principal risks disclosed in the annual report include geopolitical and macroeconomic
uncertainty affecting client technology budgets, currency risk given the large
proportion of revenue denominated in foreign currencies, talent attrition and wage
inflation risk, and the risk that generative AI adoption could compress traditional
services pricing over the medium term even as it creates new engagement categories.

# Guidance

Consistent with past practice, TCS does not provide explicit forward revenue or margin
guidance in its annual report, though management indicated a broad expectation of
continued mid-to-high single digit constant currency revenue growth over the medium
term, assuming a stable macroeconomic environment.
