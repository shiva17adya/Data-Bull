# Business Segments

This disclosure relates to an expansion of manufacturing capacity within the New Energy
business unit, which currently sits within the "Others" segment of the company's
consolidated financial statements. The board has approved an additional capital
allocation of INR 18,500 crore for the construction of a second solar module
manufacturing facility.

# Capex

The approved capital expenditure of INR 18,500 crore is incremental to previously
disclosed new energy capital expenditure plans and will be funded through a combination
of internal accruals and project-level debt financing. Construction is expected to begin
in the current fiscal year, with commercial production targeted within 30 months.

# Management Outlook

Management stated that this expansion is consistent with the company's stated ambition
to become a globally significant player in renewable energy manufacturing, and that the
additional capacity will support both domestic demand and export opportunities as global
solar module demand continues to grow.

# Risks

The disclosure notes standard execution risks associated with large greenfield
manufacturing projects, including construction delays, cost overruns, supply chain
constraints for specialized equipment, and the risk that global solar module pricing
could decline faster than currently anticipated due to oversupply from other
manufacturers.
